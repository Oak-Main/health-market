Subsidy Dropoff

Find the dollar that costs you everything.

The enhanced premium tax credits lapsed on 31 December 2025. Earning one dollar over 400% of the federal poverty line no longer reduces your health-insurance subsidy — it removes all of it. For a couple in their sixties that can be more than $17,000 a year.

This tool finds your exact number, tells you how much headroom you have left, and shows the arithmetic behind it.

Rule status as of November 2026: the enhanced credits lapsed 31 December 2025 and Congress has not enacted an extension, so the 400% FPL cap applies to the 2026 and 2027 estimates below.

2027 coverage — Open Enrollment dates

For 2027 coverage on HealthCare.gov, open enrollment runs from November 1, 2026 through January 15, 2027. Choose a plan by December 15, 2026 for January 1 coverage. Later selections through January 15 start February 1. Pay your first premium on time for coverage to begin.

On June 12, 2026, the U.S. District Court for Maryland vacated the provision that would have shortened open enrollment. The Fourth Circuit heard the government’s appeal on October 30, 2026. Its public opinions list showed no decision in the case when checked on November 5, 2026. A later ruling could change the dates.
