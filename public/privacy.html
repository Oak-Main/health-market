Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026. This policy explains what Subsidy Dropoff (https://subsidydropoff.com), operated by Oak and Main Developers LLC, collects when you visit, and what you can do about it.

What we collect

We do not ask you to create an account. When you visit, our hosting and security provider (Cloudflare) processes technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you request, to deliver and protect the site. We use the following analytics tools to understand which pages are read:

  • Google Analytics 4 (Google LLC)
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics (Cloudflare, Inc.)

These tools may set cookies or similar identifiers and collect usage data such as pages viewed, approximate location derived from IP address, device type and referring site.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4, loaded through Google's gtag.js script, to count visits and see which pages are read. It sets first-party cookies (_ga and _ga_<ID>) that hold a random identifier, and it records your approximate location, device and browser type, the page that referred you and the pages you view. We do not use it to identify you and we do not sell this data. You can block these cookies in your browser settings or use Google's opt-out at https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

Affiliate links

This site does not use affiliate links and is not paid for any product or service it mentions. If that changes, this page will say so first and those links will be labelled.

How we use information

We use this information to run, secure and improve the site and to understand which content is useful. We do not sell your personal information.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including California under the CCPA as amended by the CPRA, you may have the right to know what personal information we hold about you, to ask us to delete or correct it, and to opt out of its sale or sharing. We do not sell personal information. To make a request, contact us at the address below; we will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.
